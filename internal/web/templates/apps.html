Good to know

  • One app can serve every install. Register each install's redirect URI with the same app, and add it in each.
  • Development mode is enough for your own accounts. A platform's app review is only needed to connect other people's.
  • Araldo asks for the least access each platform needs to post, or to read ad results.
  • Deleting an app leaves its channels working until their tokens need renewing; then they sign in again through another app.
