Writing

  • # Heading, ## Smaller
  • **bold**, *italic*, [a link](https://…)
  • - item or 1. item for lists, > quote
  • --- alone for a divider
  • ![what it shows](media_…) alone for an image from the media library, or an https:// address
  • [Start a build](https://…){.button} alone for a button

Best practices

  • One main action. A single button gets more clicks than several links.
  • Subjects under 50 characters read whole on phones; preview text finishes the thought.
  • Describe every image; many readers see them blocked at first.
  • Send yourself a test and read it on a phone before scheduling.
  • Moving is safe until a copy is sent: Araldo moves it at each provider too.
{{else if and $is (eq $is.Status "pending_approval")}}

Reviewing

  • Read the preview as subscribers will: light, then “In dark mode”, then “At phone width”.
  • Send yourself a test to see it in a real inbox before approving.
  • Check the subject and the first lines: they are all most people read before deciding to open it.
  • Approve and it sends at its time; past its time, it sends within minutes.
  • Reject to send it back to draft, with a note saying what to change.
    • {{if $d.FromTemplate}}
  • Made from a template: to change every issue like it, edit {{$d.FromTemplate.Key}}.
    • {{end}}
{{else if and $is (or (eq $is.Status "scheduled") (eq $is.Status "sending"))}}

What happens next

  • A day before its time, Araldo hands each copy to its provider, scheduled to send then.
  • Moving or canceling is safe until a copy is sent: Araldo changes it at each provider too.
  • To change the text, take it back to draft; it then needs approval again.
  • Send yourself a test meanwhile if you have not.
{{else if and $is (or (eq $is.Status "sent") (eq $is.Status "partially_sent"))}}

Results

  • Araldo reads them from each provider an hour after sending, then after 1, 3, 7 and 30 days.
  • Clicks are the signal to watch. Opens are inflated by mail apps that load images on their own.
  • Unsubscribes are handled by the provider; Araldo only counts them.
