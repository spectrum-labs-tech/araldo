Authenticate the sending domain

In Brevo, under Senders, Domains & Dedicated IPs, add your newsletter subdomain and publish the records it shows:

  • DKIM: the TXT or CNAME record Brevo gives, so receivers can check the mail is yours.
  • SPF: include Brevo in the subdomain's v=spf1 record.
  • DMARC: start with v=DMARC1; p=none on the subdomain, then tighten it once reports look clean.
{{end}}

Good to know

  • Judge by clicks, not opens: mail apps that load images on their own inflate opens.
  • One provider per subscriber. Araldo cannot see addresses, so someone on lists at two providers gets two copies.
  • Unsubscribes stay with the provider. Each copy carries the provider's own unsubscribe link.
  • Links are tagged utm_medium=email with the issue, so signups show on the Performance page next to posts and ads.
  • Moving providers is cheap: export the list there, import it at the new one, connect it, and keep writing here.
