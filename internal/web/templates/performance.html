Good to know

  • Counts, never visitors. Araldo reads totals by link tag; nothing about a person reaches it.
  • Untagged traffic (search, direct visits, links typed by hand) is counted but credited to no post.
  • Recent days settle: analytics revise them, and Araldo re-reads the last week daily.
  • Visitors add up by day: someone who came on two days counts twice.
